1. INTRODUCTION

Effective Date: 24 September 2026

Soft Alliance and Resources Limited ("Soft Alliance", "we", "our", "us", or "the Company") takes the privacy and protection of Personal Data seriously. This Privacy Policy applies to information collected from or about You through SoftAce (collectively, the "Service"). By accessing or using SoftAce, You ("User" or "Authorized User") acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy (the "Privacy Policy" or "Document").

SoftAce is a web-based document management and collaboration platform that enables authorized users to upload, organize, store, access, share, copy, move, rename, and manage electronic files and folders. It also supports document collaboration, user management, role management, and access permissions.

The purpose of this Privacy Policy is to help You understand the Personal Data collected from or about You through the SoftAce platform, how it is used, disclosed, stored, and protected, and the choices available to You.

This Privacy Policy also describes Your privacy rights and how applicable law protects You. By accessing or using the Service, You acknowledge that You have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. DEFINITIONS

  • Account means a unique account or profile created for You to access SoftAce.
  • Authorized User means an individual whose Account has been created by an organization administrator and who has been assigned permission to access and use the Service.
  • Cookies means small files placed on Your Device to support authentication, security, preferences, and Service functionality.
  • Data Controller means the person or organization that determines the purposes and means of processing Personal Data.
  • Data Processor means a person or organization that processes Personal Data on behalf of a Data Controller.
  • Data Subject means the identified or identifiable individual to whom Personal Data relates.
  • Device means a computer, mobile phone, tablet, or other device used to access the Service.
  • Document Content means files, documents, folders, messages, and related information uploaded, stored, shared, or managed through the Service.
  • NDPA means the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • NDPC means the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
  • Personal Data means information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, including but not limited to identity, contact, Account, and Usage Data, and such document, sharing, and activity data that relates to, identifies, or is associated with an individual user.
  • Service means the SoftAce document management and collaboration application available through softace.softalliance.com.
  • Service Provider means a third party engaged to support the hosting, operation, security, communications, authentication, maintenance, or delivery of the Service.
  • Usage Data means technical and activity information collected automatically when the Service is accessed or used.
  • You or Your means the individual accessing or using the Service.

4. OBTAINING AND USING PERSONAL DATA

4.1 Categories of Data Collected and Purpose of Processing

To provide and secure the features of SoftAce, we may collect and process the following categories of Personal Data:

  • Full Name: Collected to identify You, create and manage Your Account, associate You with Your organization or department, and administer access to the Service.
  • Email Address: Collected for login, Account administration, password recovery, document-sharing notifications, security communications, and support.
  • Account Credentials: Your email address or username, encrypted password, authentication information, access rights, and login records are processed to authenticate You and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Organizational Information: Your organization, department, job title, and assigned role may be processed to manage access, document sharing, and collaboration.
  • Uploaded Files and Document Content: Files and documents uploaded to SoftAce are processed to store, organize, retrieve, manage, and share content as instructed by authorized users.
  • File and Folder Metadata: File names, folder names, file types, sizes, upload dates, ownership, storage locations, and related metadata are processed to organize and manage the document repository.
  • Document-Sharing and Collaboration Information: Selected users or departments, access levels, sharing messages, and collaboration records are processed to enable authorized document sharing and collaboration.
  • User Roles and Permissions: Role names, descriptions, assigned permissions, and access levels are processed to control access to documents, folders, and application functions.
  • Document Activity and Audit Logs: Records of uploads, views, shares, copies, moves, renames, changes, login events, and timestamps are processed to maintain accountability, investigate incidents, prevent misuse, and support compliance.
  • Technical Information: IP address, Device information, browser type, operating system, access time, and system logs may be collected to operate and secure the Service, troubleshoot issues, and improve performance.
  • Support Requests and Correspondence: Collected when You request technical assistance, submit an inquiry, report an issue, or provide feedback.
  • Other Necessary Information: Other Personal Data may be processed where necessary to provide the Service or comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

4.2 Sources of Data

  • Directly from You: Information provided when You log in, update Your Account, upload or manage files, create folders, share documents, or contact support.
  • Your Organization: Identity, employment, department, role, and access information provided by the organization that authorizes Your use of SoftAce.
  • Administrators and Other Authorized Users: Information entered when administrators create Accounts or roles, assign permissions, or when users share documents and collaboration messages with You.
  • Integrated Systems: Information received from authorized identity-management, communications, storage, or other systems connected to SoftAce.
  • Service Providers: Technical, authentication, hosting, security, communications, maintenance, and support information received from authorized providers.
  • Automated Technologies: Technical and Usage Data collected through cookies, access logs, security-monitoring tools, and similar technologies.

4.3 Automatically Collected Data and Cookies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain user sessions, support authentication, remember permitted preferences, protect the Service, monitor usage, and improve functionality. Technical information such as IP address, browser type, Device information, operating system, access time, and activity logs may be collected automatically when You use SoftAce. Where required by law, non-essential cookies will be used only after obtaining Your consent.

For additional information, please read our Cookie Policy.

5. DISCLOSURE AND THIRD-PARTY DATA SHARING

We do not sell Your Personal Data. We may disclose Personal Data where necessary to provide the Service, follow Your organization's authorized instructions, enable document collaboration, protect the Service, or comply with applicable law. Personal Data may be shared with:

  • Your Organization and Authorized Users: Administrators, employees, departments, and other users authorized to access Your Account information or receive documents shared through the Service.
  • Technology and Hosting Providers: Trusted providers supporting hosting, storage, security, authentication, communications, technical maintenance, and other functions necessary to operate SoftAce.
  • Integrated System Providers: Authorized providers of identity management, communications, storage, or other systems connected to the Service.
  • Professional Advisers and Auditors: Auditors, lawyers, consultants, and other advisers where access is necessary for authorized duties.
  • Legal and Regulatory Authorities: Government, regulatory, judicial, or law-enforcement authorities where disclosure is required by law, court order, or another lawful request.

Recipients of Personal Data must process it only for authorized purposes and apply appropriate confidentiality and security safeguards.

6. DATA STORAGE, RETENTION AND LOCATION

  • Data Storage: Personal Data and Document Content processed through SoftAce are stored on authorized systems and infrastructure using appropriate technical and organizational safeguards.
  • Cross-Border Transfers: Where Personal Data is transferred outside Nigeria, the transfer will comply with applicable provisions of the NDPA and be protected by appropriate legal, contractual, and security safeguards.
  • Retention: Personal Data, files, folders, sharing records, permissions, and audit logs are retained only for as long as necessary to provide the Service, follow the relevant organization's instructions, maintain security and accountability, resolve disputes, and comply with legal or regulatory obligations. At the end of the applicable retention period, the information will be securely deleted, anonymized, or returned to the relevant Data Controller, subject to contractual, legal, technical, and backup requirements.

7. TECHNICAL SECURITY AND CONFIDENTIALITY

We implement commercially reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect Personal Data and Document Content processed through SoftAce. These safeguards include encryption in transit, access controls, authentication mechanisms, role-based permissions, controlled document sharing, activity logging, security monitoring, and backup procedures designed to maintain confidentiality, integrity, and availability. Access is restricted to authorized persons with legitimate responsibilities.

8. LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING YOUR PERSONAL DATA

Under Section 25 of the NDPA 2023, Personal Data is processed through SoftAce on one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a Contract: Where processing is necessary to provide the Service, create and manage Accounts, store and manage documents, enable collaboration, provide support, or take steps at Your request.
  • Compliance with Legal Obligations: Where processing is necessary to comply with audit, cybersecurity, record-retention, regulatory, court-order, or other legal obligations.
  • Legitimate Interests: Where processing is necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and improve SoftAce, prevent misuse, troubleshoot issues, maintain audit records, and protect organizational information, provided these interests do not override Your rights and freedoms.
  • Consent: Where required, we rely on Your consent for a specified activity, including non-essential cookies or optional communications. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.
  • Public Interest or Official Authority: Where an authorized public body uses the Service and processing is necessary to perform a task in the public interest or exercise official authority.

Subject to the NDPA 2023 and applicable legal limitations, You may exercise the following rights:

  • The right to request access to Your Personal Data and information about its processing.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete Personal Data.
  • The right to request deletion of Personal Data where there is no lawful reason for continued processing.
  • The right to object to or request restriction of processing where permitted by law.
  • The right to withdraw consent without affecting processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.
  • The right to receive or request the transfer of Your Personal Data where applicable.
  • The right not to be subject to certain decisions based solely on automated processing where applicable.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
  • Any other right available under the NDPA 2023 or applicable regulations and guidelines.

Where Your organization is the Data Controller, requests concerning documents, employee records, sharing permissions, or other information controlled by that organization should ordinarily be submitted to the organization. Soft Alliance will provide reasonable assistance to the relevant Data Controller in responding to valid requests.
