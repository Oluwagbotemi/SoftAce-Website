2. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They enable a website to recognise your device, remember certain information about your preferences, support functionality, and, in some cases, understand how visitors interact with the website.

Cookies may be:

  • Session cookies: These are temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies: These remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them.
  • First-party cookies: These are placed by the website you are visiting.
  • Third-party cookies: These are placed by third-party service providers whose services are integrated into the website.

We may also use technologies similar to cookies, such as pixels, web beacons, tags, local storage and software development kits (SDKs), where applicable.

3. WHY WE USE COOKIES

We may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Support login, authentication and session management;
  • Improve the performance, functionality and usability of the website;
  • Understand how visitors use the website;
  • Measure website traffic and engagement;
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve our services; and
  • Support marketing or advertising activities, where applicable and permitted by law.

We do not use cookies for purposes incompatible with those disclosed in this Policy.
